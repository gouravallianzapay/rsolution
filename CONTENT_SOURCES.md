## Audit decisions

Existing copy was reorganized and shortened. Conflicting client counts, aging business-year counters, unverified certification labels, Google rating/follower counters and unclear placement logos were omitted. Duplicate course bullets were removed. Service outputs are described without inventing specifications or guaranteed accuracy. Proposed process is labeled as suggested. Industries reflect applications listed in the original service copy, not verified engagements.

The published Sachin Joshi testimonial is a short excerpt, linked to its source. Other published reviewers are identified with links rather than new quotations. No star ratings were normalized or added.
